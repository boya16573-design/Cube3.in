# Third-party software

Math Canvas contains these libraries, copied into `index.html` so the page works as one file. Both are MIT licensed. Their copyright notices and licence text are below, as the licence requires.

- **mp4-muxer** 5.1.3, by Vanilagy: writes MP4 video files.
- **webm-muxer** 5.0.3, by Vanilagy: writes WebM video files.

The page also asks Google Fonts for typefaces when they are available. Those fonts have their own open licences (SIL Open Font License or Apache 2.0).
