How this works, and what it is not

What it is. A design and photo/video editor where every effect is a formula you can read. Pick any layer and open the Check tab: you will see the exact equations behind the look, and a quality test that measures your photo or video (resolution, sharpness, noise, exposure, colour, text contrast).

Real maths, on your real files. Brightness, contrast, blur and glow are computed in linear light, the way real light adds up. Photos shrink with Lanczos resampling. Videos export frame by frame, so nothing is dropped. “Measure export error” compares the preview to the export and shows the number.

What it is not. It does not use AI to invent photos or video. The “realistic scenes” and manga looks are drawn from equations, so they are stylised, not photographs. Nothing can promise a “perfect” result, so the app measures and shows you where a problem is.

Your files stay with you. Everything runs in your browser. Projects save on this device, and “Save file” makes a copy you can move.

Realistic scenes

Landscapes drawn from equations: sky, sun, clouds, mountains, haze and a lake. They are procedural, so they are made-up places, not photos. Use one as a background, then add your text or subject on top. Press play to see it move.

Seconds per caption
Look

“Time to speech” measures when someone is talking in your video or sound (from the real loudness, every 20 ms) and places each line there. You still supply the words. “Dictate” uses your browser’s speech recognition and the microphone, so it needs Chrome or Edge and an allowed microphone.

Music

MP3, WAV, M4A or OGG. It is mixed into the exported video together with the video’s own sound.

3D math shapes

Real 3D from your own equations x(u, v), y(u, v), z(u, v): orbit it, move it, measure it, and see exact error reports. Exact area and volume are shown only where the shape's formulas apply.

Graphs and calculus

Type any formula. You get the graph, its slope (derivative), the area under it (integral) and where it hits zero, all worked out live.

Math art

Each one is drawn from equations. Use it as a background or an accent, then change its numbers in Edit.
