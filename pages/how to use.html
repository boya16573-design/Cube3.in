How to use Cube3

A quick walkthrough, whether you're a creator or a brand.

1

Create your account

Sign up and choose "Creator." You'll get a 6-digit code by email to confirm your account.

2

Complete your profile

Go to Profile → Edit profile. Add a photo, bio, your handle, category, and links to your social accounts with follower counts. A complete profile gets noticed more.

3

Browse campaigns

Go to Campaigns to see what brands are looking for. Use search and filters to find ones that match your niche.

4

Apply

Tap "Apply now" on a campaign, write a short pitch, and optionally add your rate and a link to your portfolio or best content.

5

Track your applications

Check "My Applications" to see if you've been Shortlisted, Selected, or Rejected.

6

Message and deliver

Once selected, Messages unlocks a chat with the brand. Check Collaborations for any deliverables they've set, and submit your content link there for approval.

7

Get reviewed

After a collaboration, you and the brand can leave each other a rating and review, which shows on your public profile.

1

Create your account

Sign up and choose "Brand." Confirm your account with the 6-digit code sent to your email.

2

Complete your profile

Add your company name, industry, description, and website so creators know who they're working with.

3

Post a campaign

Go to Campaigns → Create campaign. Use the 4-step wizard (or start from a template) to set your budget, deliverables, and timeline.

4

Browse creators directly

Don't want to wait for applications? Go to Creators, search by category, and message anyone directly — no application needed.

5

Review applications

Go to Applications to see who applied, their pitch, rate, and portfolio. Shortlist your favorites, then Select the ones you want.

6

Track deliverables

In Collaborations, add specific milestones (e.g. "1 Instagram Reel") for each selected creator. Approve or reject their submitted content.

7

Check your results

Analytics shows your campaign performance — applications received, acceptance rate, and ratings — with CSV export.

8

Leave a review

Rate the creator after your collaboration wraps up — it helps other brands and builds the creator's reputation.
